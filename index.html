Our Mission

Investing in the people who make Vero Beach home

Quail Valley Charities strengthens Indian River County by providing financial support, volunteer resources, and lasting partnerships to the organizations and individuals who need it most. We believe philanthropy works best close to home — rooted in relationships, guided by need, and measured by real outcomes for local families.

Our vision: a Vero Beach where every family has access to opportunity, quality care, and a community that shows up for one another.
